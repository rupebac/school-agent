A clear title, a useful message

Child first. What changed or what matters next. A deadline that is clearly different from the event date.

  • Short bold headings and breathing room.
  • One link to the matter and its source.
  • No scores, assignments, or task numbering.
  • Information says “No reply needed” only when that is actually supported.
  • Existing quiet hours and notification choices stay.
